Narezki Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

What we collect. When you connect a TikTok account, Narezki receives an access token and basic profile information (account ID, display name, avatar). When you publish, the app sends your video, caption and posting settings to TikTok.

Where data is stored. All data, including tokens, videos and settings, is stored only on your own computer. Narezki has no servers and does not send your data to us or to any third party, except the platforms you choose to publish to.

How data is used. Data is used only to publish videos you select and to show your publishing status and statistics inside the app. We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Deleting data. You can disconnect your account in the app, which deletes the stored token, and revoke access in your TikTok settings. Uninstalling the app and deleting its folder removes all local data.
