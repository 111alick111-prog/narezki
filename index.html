Narezki

Narezki is a desktop application for Windows that helps content creators turn long videos into short vertical clips. It finds interesting moments, adds subtitles and captions, and publishes the clips to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts through their official APIs.

The app runs locally on the user's computer. Videos are processed on the user's device and are uploaded only to accounts the user has connected and authorized.
